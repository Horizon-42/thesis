3. 起点：平直 ENU 切平面里的点质量动力学Start: Point-Mass Dynamics in a Flat ENU Tangent Plane

前置文档已经把 ENU 这个坐标系定义清楚了：选一个原点 \(O_L\)，三根本地正交轴 \(\hat{\mathbf e}_0\)（东）、\(\hat{\mathbf n}_{N,0}\)（北）、\(\hat{\mathbf u}_0\)（天）。make_dynamics_model 就是在这块平面里写牛顿运动，状态 \((x,y,h,V,\psi,\gamma,m)\)。 The prerequisite document already defined the ENU frame: pick an origin \(O_L\) and three local orthonormal axes \(\hat{\mathbf e}_0\) (East), \(\hat{\mathbf n}_{N,0}\) (North), \(\hat{\mathbf u}_0\) (Up). make_dynamics_model simply writes Newtonian motion inside that plane, with state \((x,y,h,V,\psi,\gamma,m)\).

状态、控制与速度分解State, Control, and Velocity Decomposition

位置 \((x,y,h)\) 是相对原点向东、向北、向上的米。速度大小是 \(V\)，方向由航向 \(\psi\)（在水平面内，\(\psi=0\) 朝东）和航迹角 \(\gamma\)（抬头爬升为正）决定。把 \(V\) 投到三轴： Position \((x,y,h)\) is in metres east, north, and up of the origin. Speed is \(V\); its direction is set by heading \(\psi\) (in the horizontal plane, \(\psi=0\) is east) and flight-path angle \(\gamma\) (positive climbing). Project \(V\) onto the three axes:

\[ V_E=V\cos\gamma\cos\psi,\quad V_N=V\cos\gamma\sin\psi,\quad V_U=V\sin\gamma \]
\(x,y,h\)：东、北、天位置（米）。East, north, up position (m).
\(V\)：空速（m/s）。Airspeed (m/s).
\(\psi\)：航向角（rad），\(0=\) 东。Heading (rad), \(0=\) east.
\(\gamma\)：航迹角（rad）。Flight-path angle (rad).

七个状态的连续 RHSThe Continuous RHS for the Seven States

位置率直接就是速度分量（平面里“走一米就是一米”）；速度与姿态率来自推力 \(T\)、阻力 \(D\)、重力 \(g\) 和载荷因子 \(n\)、坡度 \(\mu\)。 Position rates are simply the velocity components (in a plane, “one metre travelled is one metre”); speed and attitude rates come from thrust \(T\), drag \(D\), gravity \(g\), load factor \(n\), and bank \(\mu\).

\[ \dot x=V\cos\gamma\cos\psi,\qquad \dot y=V\cos\gamma\sin\psi,\qquad \dot h=V\sin\gamma \] \[ \dot V=\frac{T-D}{m}-g\sin\gamma \] \[ \dot\psi=\frac{g\,n\sin\mu}{V\cos\gamma},\qquad \dot\gamma=\frac{g\,(n\cos\mu-\cos\gamma)}{V} \]

Note: \(\dot x,\dot y\) are exactly \(V_E,V_N\). This is the part that must change when we leave the flat plane.

4. 为什么“平面”不够：切平面会脱离椭球Why “Flat” Is Not Enough: The Tangent Plane Peels Off the Ellipsoid

ENU 平面只在原点处和椭球相切。沿平面飞远了，平面会越来越“高出”真实地表。把水平距离记作 \(L\)、地球半径量级记作 \(R\approx6.37\times10^6\,\mathrm m\)，竖向偏离可估计为 \(\epsilon_U\approx L^2/2R\)。拖动下面滑块看它怎么长大。 The ENU plane is tangent to the ellipsoid only at the origin. Fly far along it and the plane rises increasingly above the true surface. Let \(L\) be the horizontal distance and \(R\approx6.37\times10^6\,\mathrm m\) the Earth-radius scale; the vertical departure is \(\epsilon_U\approx L^2/2R\). Drag the slider to watch it grow.

5. 数学预备：WGS 84 的两个曲率半径 \(R_M,R_N\)Math Primer: The Two WGS 84 Radii of Curvature \(R_M,R_N\)

这一节要算两个“曲率半径” \(R_M,R_N\)——它们是第 6 节把“走了几米”换成“纬度/经度变了几弧度”的换算系数。但在用它们之前，得先真正搞懂：地球这个“椭球”是什么形状？公式里到处都是的那个 \(W=\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\phi}\) 又是从哪冒出来的？下面假设你只学过初中数学，从圆和椭圆一步步来。 This section computes the two “radii of curvature” \(R_M,R_N\) — the factors Section 6 uses to turn “metres travelled” into “radians of latitude/longitude.” But before using them, we should really understand: what shape is this “ellipsoid”? And where does that \(W=\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\phi}\), which appears everywhere, come from? Below, assuming only middle-school maths, we build it up from circles and ellipses.

第 0 步：圆 → 椭圆（认识 \(a,b,f,e^2\)）Step 0: Circle → Ellipse (meet \(a,b,f,e^2\))

一个圆：所有点到圆心的距离都等于 \(a\)。把它在竖直方向压扁（乘以一个小于 1 的比例 \(b/a\)），就得到椭圆。地球绕自转轴转，被“甩”得赤道略鼓、两极略扁，所以它的子午剖面（切开看的纵截面）就是这样一个椭圆。 A circle: every point is distance \(a\) from the centre. Squash it vertically (multiply by a ratio \(b/a<1\)) and you get an ellipse. The spinning Earth bulges at the equator and flattens at the poles, so its meridian cross-section is exactly such an ellipse.

\[ \frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{z^2}{b^2}=1 \]
\(x\)：离自转轴的水平距离。horizontal distance from the spin axis.
\(z\)：沿自转轴的高度。height along the spin axis.
\(a\)：半长轴 = 赤道半径 = 6378.137 km。semi-major axis = equatorial radius.
\(b\)：半短轴 = 极半径 = 6356.752 km。semi-minor axis = polar radius.

两个“扁”的度量：扁率 \(f=\dfrac{a-b}{a}\approx\dfrac1{298}\)；偏心率平方 \(e^2=1-\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}=f(2-f)\approx0.0067\)。都很小——地球其实非常接近正球（\(a,b\) 只差 21 km）。 Two measures of “flatness”: flattening \(f=\dfrac{a-b}{a}\approx\dfrac1{298}\); eccentricity-squared \(e^2=1-\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}=f(2-f)\approx0.0067\). Both tiny — Earth is very nearly a sphere (\(a,b\) differ by only 21 km).

第 1 步：大地纬度 \(\phi\) 是“法线”的角度，不是“中心连线”的角度Step 1: Geodetic Latitude \(\phi\) Is the Angle of the Normal, Not the Centre Line

我们平时说的“纬度”，是当地铅垂方向（垂直于地面、也就是椭圆的法线）和赤道面的夹角——叫大地纬度 \(\phi\)。它不是“从地心连到你”的那条线和赤道面的夹角（那个叫地心纬度 \(\psi\)）。 The everyday “latitude” is the angle between the local vertical (perpendicular to the ground = the ellipse normal) and the equatorial plane — the geodetic latitude \(\phi\). It is not the angle of the line from the Earth’s centre to you (that is the geocentric latitude \(\psi\)).

在正球上，法线正好穿过球心，两者相同。但椭圆被压扁后，法线一般不穿过中心（只有赤道和两极例外），所以 \(\phi\ne\psi\)（在地球上只差零点几度，但这是后面一切的根源）。下图把这个区别画出来——形状故意夸张了才看得清。 On a sphere the normal passes through the centre, so the two agree. But once the ellipse is squashed, the normal generally misses the centre (except at the equator and poles), so \(\phi\ne\psi\) (only a fraction of a degree on Earth, but the root of everything later). 第 2 步：\(W\) 是怎么冒出来的（只用初中代数，一步都不跳）Step 2: Where \(W\) Comes From (middle-school algebra, no skipped steps)

目标：只用初中代数，从椭圆推出 \(W\) 和 \(N=a/W\)。下面拆成 5 个小步，每步只做一件事。 Goal: with only middle-school algebra, derive \(W\) and \(N=a/W\) from the ellipse. Split into 5 micro-steps, each doing exactly one thing.

2.1 先搞清“法线方向”长什么样2.1 First, what does the “normal direction” look like?

法线 = 垂直于切线的方向；切线 = 在 P 点“擦着碰一下”的那条直线。我们要的是 P 处指向外面的那条垂线方向。用两个初中工具就能拿到它： The normal is the direction perpendicular to the tangent; the tangent is the straight line that just grazes the curve at P. We want the outward perpendicular at P. Two middle-school tools give it:

  • 工具一（直线的法线）：直线 \(A\,x+B\,z=C\)，和它垂直的方向就是把系数拼起来的 \((A,B)\)。例：直线 \(3x+4z=5\) 的垂直方向是 \((3,4)\)。Tool 1 (a line’s normal): the line \(A\,x+B\,z=C\) is perpendicular to the direction \((A,B)\) made from its coefficients. E.g. \(3x+4z=5\) is perpendicular to \((3,4)\).
  • 工具二（切线公式）：圆 \(x^2+z^2=R^2\) 在点 \((x_0,z_0)\) 的切线是 \(x_0x+z_0z=R^2\)（法线 \((x_0,z_0)\)，正是“圆心指向该点”，符合直觉）。椭圆只是把 \(x^2\) 换成 \(\frac{x^2}{a^2}\)：切线是 \(\dfrac{x_0}{a^2}x+\dfrac{z_0}{b^2}z=1\)。Tool 2 (the tangent formula): a circle \(x^2+z^2=R^2\) at \((x_0,z_0)\) has tangent \(x_0x+z_0z=R^2\) (normal \((x_0,z_0)\) = “centre-to-point”, as expected). The ellipse just replaces \(x^2\) by \(\frac{x^2}{a^2}\): the tangent is \(\dfrac{x_0}{a^2}x+\dfrac{z_0}{b^2}z=1\).

把工具一用到这条切线上：它的系数是 \(\big(\frac{x_0}{a^2},\frac{z_0}{b^2}\big)\)，所以椭圆在 P 的法线方向就是 \(\big(\frac{x_0}{a^2},\frac{z_0}{b^2}\big)\)。下图把这件事画出来： Apply Tool 1 to that tangent: its coefficients are \(\big(\frac{x_0}{a^2},\frac{z_0}{b^2}\big)\), so the ellipse’s normal direction at P is \(\big(\frac{x_0}{a^2},\frac{z_0}{b^2}\big)\). 2.2 大地纬度把法线方向“钉”成 \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\)2.2 Geodetic latitude pins the normal to \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\)

先约定记号（这点容易混）：我们关心的那个固定点记作 \(P=(x_0,z_0)\)。注意 2.1 切线方程 \(\frac{x_0}{a^2}x+\frac{z_0}{b^2}z=1\) 里的 \((x,z)\) 是切线上的流动坐标（动点），和固定点 \(P\) 不是一回事——它只在写“那条直线”时出现，下面不再用到。从这里起，所有运算只针对固定点，一律写带下标的 \((x_0,z_0)\)。\(P\) 的法线方向就是 \(\bigl(\tfrac{x_0}{a^2},\tfrac{z_0}{b^2}\bigr)\)。 Fix the notation first (this is the easy-to-confuse part): the fixed point we care about is \(P=(x_0,z_0)\). In the 2.1 tangent equation \(\frac{x_0}{a^2}x+\frac{z_0}{b^2}z=1\), the \((x,z)\) are the running coordinates of the tangent line (a moving point), not the point \(P\) — they appear only to write “that line” and are not used again. From here on every step is about the fixed point, written with subscripts \((x_0,z_0)\). Its normal direction is \(\bigl(\tfrac{x_0}{a^2},\tfrac{z_0}{b^2}\bigr)\).

回忆第 1 步：\(\phi\) 就是法线和水平的夹角。一个与水平成 \(\phi\) 角、长度为 1 的方向，写出来正是 \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\)。既然 \(P\) 的法线方向 \(\big(\frac{x_0}{a^2},\frac{z_0}{b^2}\big)\) 和 \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\) 指向同一个方向，它们必成比例。设比例为 \(k\)： Recall Step 1: \(\phi\) is the angle between the normal and horizontal. A unit direction at angle \(\phi\) from horizontal is exactly \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\). Since \(P\)’s normal \(\big(\frac{x_0}{a^2},\frac{z_0}{b^2}\big)\) points the same way as \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\), they must be proportional. Call the ratio \(k\):

\[ \frac{x_0}{a^2}=k\cos\phi,\qquad \frac{z_0}{b^2}=k\sin\phi \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x_0=k\,a^2\cos\phi,\quad z_0=k\,b^2\sin\phi \]

（右边只是两边各乘回 \(a^2\)、\(b^2\) 消掉分母。\(k\) 还不知道，下一步定它。）(The right side just multiplies both sides by \(a^2\), \(b^2\) to clear denominators. \(k\) is still unknown — pinned next.)

2.3 用“P 在椭圆上”把 \(k\) 算出来2.3 Use “P lies on the ellipse” to find \(k\)

固定点 \(P=(x_0,z_0)\) 在椭圆上，必满足 \(\frac{x_0^2}{a^2}+\frac{z_0^2}{b^2}=1\)。把 2.2 的 \(x_0,z_0\) 代进去，一项一项算：The fixed point \(P=(x_0,z_0)\) is on the ellipse, so \(\frac{x_0^2}{a^2}+\frac{z_0^2}{b^2}=1\). Substitute the \(x_0,z_0\) from 2.2, term by term:

\[ \frac{x_0^2}{a^2}=\frac{(k a^2\cos\phi)^2}{a^2}=\frac{k^2a^4\cos^2\phi}{a^2}=k^2a^2\cos^2\phi \] \[ \frac{z_0^2}{b^2}=\frac{(k b^2\sin\phi)^2}{b^2}=k^2b^2\sin^2\phi \]

两式相加 \(=1\)，把 \(k^2\) 提出来，就能解出 \(k\)：Add the two \(=1\), factor out \(k^2\), and solve for \(k\):

\[ k^2\bigl(a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi\bigr)=1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi}} \]

2.4 化简那个根号——\(W\) 就在这里冒出来2.4 Simplify that root — \(W\) is born here

盯住根号里的 \(a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi\)，三步整理：Stare at \(a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi\) inside the root; tidy it in three moves:

\[ a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi \;\overset{(1)}{=}\;a^2\Bigl(\cos^2\phi+\tfrac{b^2}{a^2}\sin^2\phi\Bigr) \;\overset{(2)}{=}\;a^2\Bigl(\cos^2\phi+(1-e^2)\sin^2\phi\Bigr) \] \[ \overset{(3)}{=}\;a^2\bigl(\underbrace{\cos^2\phi+\sin^2\phi}_{=\,1}-\,e^2\sin^2\phi\bigr) =a^2\bigl(1-e^2\sin^2\phi\bigr) \]

（(1) 提出 \(a^2\)；(2) 用第 0 步的 \(\frac{b^2}{a^2}=1-e^2\)；(3) 展开后用 \(\cos^2\phi+\sin^2\phi=1\)。）开方：((1) factor out \(a^2\); (2) use Step 0’s \(\frac{b^2}{a^2}=1-e^2\); (3) expand and use \(\cos^2\phi+\sin^2\phi=1\).) Take the square root:

\[ \sqrt{a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi}=a\,\underbrace{\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\phi}}_{=\,W} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \boxed{\,W=\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\phi}\,} \]

所以 \(W\) 不是凭空规定的——它就是把 \(a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi\) 整理后剩下的那个根号因子。So \(W\) is not arbitrary — it is just the leftover square-root factor after tidying \(a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi\).

2.5 代回，得到 \(P\) 的坐标（先把 \(a/W\) 简记为 \(N\)）2.5 Substitute back for \(P\)’s coordinates (abbreviate \(a/W\) as \(N\))

由 \(k=\dfrac1{\sqrt{a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi}}=\dfrac1{aW}\)（用了 2.4 的根号 \(=aW\)），代回 2.2 的 \(x_0\)：From \(k=\dfrac1{\sqrt{a^2\cos^2\phi+b^2\sin^2\phi}}=\dfrac1{aW}\) (using 2.4’s root \(=aW\)), substitute back into 2.2’s \(x_0\):

\[ x_0=k\,a^2\cos\phi=\frac{a^2\cos\phi}{a\,W}=\frac{a}{W}\cos\phi =\underbrace{\frac{a}{W}}_{=:\,N}\cos\phi, \qquad z_0=\frac{a}{W}(1-e^2)\sin\phi \]

这里 \(N:=\dfrac{a}{W}\) 暂时只是 \(a/W\) 的缩写——还没说它等于 \(R_N\)。我们得到的是固定点 \(P\) 的两个坐标，其中横坐标 \(x_0=N\cos\phi\) 就是 \(P\) 离自转轴的水平距离。 Here \(N:=\dfrac{a}{W}\) is so far only an abbreviation for \(a/W\) — we have not yet claimed it equals \(R_N\). What we obtained are \(P\)’s two coordinates; the horizontal one \(x_0=N\cos\phi\) is \(P\)’s horizontal distance from the spin axis.

2.6 \(R_N\) 是什么？——把 \(R_N=a/W\) 推出来（不是定义）2.6 What is \(R_N\)? — derive \(R_N=a/W\) (not define it)

到上一步为止 \(N\) 只是缩写。\(R_N\)（卯酉圈半径）有它自己独立的几何身份，不是我们随手命名的：它是 \(P\) 处法线一直延伸到自转轴的那段长度（图 2a 里的蓝色粗段）。我们把这段长度算出来，看它到底等于多少——这才是“推导”。 Up to here \(N\) is just shorthand. \(R_N\) (the prime-vertical radius) has its own independent geometric identity, not something we just named: it is the length of the normal from \(P\) extended to the spin axis (the thick blue segment in Figure 2a). Let us compute that length and see what it equals — that is the actual derivation.

从 \(P=(x_0,z_0)\) 沿法线方向 \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\) 往里走，直到横坐标变成 \(0\)（碰到自转轴）。横向一共要走的距离正好是 \(x_0\)；而法线与水平成 \(\phi\) 角，所以这段斜的法线长度 \(\lvert PF\rvert\) 满足“横向 \(=\) 斜边 \(\times\cos\phi\)”： From \(P=(x_0,z_0)\), travel inward along the normal \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\) until the horizontal coordinate hits \(0\) (the spin axis). The horizontal distance to cover is exactly \(x_0\); the normal makes angle \(\phi\) with horizontal, so its length \(\lvert PF\rvert\) obeys “horizontal \(=\) slant \(\times\cos\phi\)”:

\[ \lvert PF\rvert\cos\phi=x_0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \lvert PF\rvert=\frac{x_0}{\cos\phi} \overset{\,x_0=(a/W)\cos\phi\,}{=}\; \frac{(a/W)\cos\phi}{\cos\phi}=\frac{a}{W} \]

\(R_N\) 的几何定义就是这段“法线到轴”的长度，而我们刚算出它 \(=a/W\)。所以 \(R_N\) is defined as this normal-to-axis length, and we just computed it \(=a/W\). Therefore

\[ \boxed{\;R_N=\lvert PF\rvert=\frac{a}{W}\;}\qquad\text{(derived, not assumed)} \]

这下上一步的缩写 \(N\) 才正式有名分：\(N=R_N=a/W\)。\(P\) 的水平距离也就写成 \(x_0=R_N\cos\phi\)（第 6 节、第 3 步都用它）。Only now does the shorthand \(N\) earn its name: \(N=R_N=a/W\). And \(P\)’s horizontal distance is \(x_0=R_N\cos\phi\) (used in Section 6 and Step 3).

所以 \(R_N=\dfrac{a}{W}\) 是结论，不是规定。 它把赤道半径 \(a\) 除以 \(W\in[\sqrt{1-e^2},\,1]\)；\(W\) 越小 \(R_N\) 越大，所以卯酉圈半径在两极最大。（更深一层：这段“法线到轴”的长度，同时也正是“卯酉法截面”的曲率半径（后面的图 2b 会画出来）——所以管它叫“曲率半径”名副其实；但那是另一个定理，算经纬度时用不到。） So \(R_N=\dfrac{a}{W}\) is a conclusion, not a definition. It divides the equatorial radius \(a\) by \(W\in[\sqrt{1-e^2},\,1]\); smaller \(W\) → larger \(R_N\), so the prime-vertical radius is largest at the poles. (One layer deeper: this normal-to-axis length is also the radius of curvature of the prime-vertical normal section (drawn later as Figure 2b) — so calling it a “radius of curvature” is fully justified; but that is a separate theorem, not needed for the lat/lon conversion.)

第 3 步：\(R_M\) 的完整推导（曲率半径 \(=\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}\)，要用一点微积分）Step 3: Full Derivation of \(R_M\) (radius of curvature \(=\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}\), with a bit of calculus)

3.1 \(R_M\) 到底是什么3.1 What \(R_M\) actually is

任意平面曲线的曲率半径定义为 \(R=\dfrac{ds}{d\theta}\)：沿曲线走一小段弧长 \(ds\)，切线方向转过 \(d\theta\)，转得越慢（越平直）半径越大。在子午线上，法线（与切线刚性垂直）的仰角正是大地纬度 \(\phi\)，法线转多少切线就转多少，于是 \(d\theta=d\phi\)。所以 For any plane curve the radius of curvature is \(R=\dfrac{ds}{d\theta}\): move an arc length \(ds\) and the tangent turns by \(d\theta\); the slower it turns (the straighter), the larger the radius. On the meridian, the normal (rigidly perpendicular to the tangent) has elevation equal to the geodetic latitude \(\phi\), so the normal and tangent turn together, \(d\theta=d\phi\). Hence

\[ R_M=\frac{ds}{d\phi} \]

直观：\(R_M\) 就是“纬度每多 1 弧度，沿子午线要走多少米”。Intuitively, \(R_M\) is “how many metres of meridian arc per radian of latitude.”

3.2 从目标出发：到底要算什么3.2 Start from the goal: what exactly we must compute

我们要的是 \(R_M=\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}\)。手上只有第 2 步给的、子午线上点 \(P\) 的两个坐标 \(p(\phi),z(\phi)\)：We want \(R_M=\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}\). All we have is point \(P\)’s two meridian coordinates \(p(\phi),z(\phi)\) from Step 2:

\[ p(\phi)=\frac{a}{W}\cos\phi,\qquad z(\phi)=\frac{a(1-e^2)}{W}\sin\phi,\qquad W=\bigl(1-e^2\sin^2\phi\bigr)^{1/2} \]

（\(p\) = 离自转轴的水平距离，\(z\) = 沿轴高度。）弧长满足 \(ds^2=dp^2+dz^2\)，于是(\(p\) = horizontal distance from the spin axis, \(z\) = height along it.) Arc length satisfies \(ds^2=dp^2+dz^2\), so

\[ \frac{ds}{d\phi}=\sqrt{\Bigl(\frac{dp}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2+\Bigl(\frac{dz}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2} \]

所以整件事只剩：把 \(p,z\) 对 \(\phi\) 求导，再代进去。下面一个一个来，途中需要哪个导数，到那一刻才去算——不提前准备。（这样才是真的“顺着推”，而不是先知道答案再倒着凑。）So the whole task is just to differentiate \(p,z\) with respect to \(\phi\) and plug in. We go one at a time and compute any sub-derivative only at the moment we hit it — not staged in advance. (That is what makes this a genuine forward derivation rather than reverse-engineering a known answer.)

3.3 求 \(\dfrac{dp}{d\phi}\)（途中自然碰到 \(\dfrac{dW}{d\phi}\)，到那时再算）3.3 Compute \(\dfrac{dp}{d\phi}\) (we hit \(\dfrac{dW}{d\phi}\) along the way — compute it then)

\(p=a\cos\phi\cdot W^{-1}\) 是两块相乘，用乘积法则 \((uv)'=u'v+uv'\)，取 \(u=a\cos\phi\)（\(u'=-a\sin\phi\)）、\(v=W^{-1}\)：\(p=a\cos\phi\cdot W^{-1}\) is a product; product rule \((uv)'=u'v+uv'\) with \(u=a\cos\phi\) (\(u'=-a\sin\phi\)), \(v=W^{-1}\):

\[ \frac{dp}{d\phi}=-a\sin\phi\cdot W^{-1}+a\cos\phi\cdot\bigl(W^{-1}\bigr)' \]

到这里卡在 \(\bigl(W^{-1}\bigr)'\)——要往下走，现在才需要知道 \(W\) 怎么随 \(\phi\) 变。于是这一刻顺手去算 \(\dfrac{dW}{d\phi}\)：\(W=(1-e^2\sin^2\phi)^{1/2}\)，链式法则；先单独求最里层 \(\dfrac{d}{d\phi}\sin^2\phi=2\sin\phi\cos\phi\)，再往外套：Now we are stuck at \(\bigl(W^{-1}\bigr)'\) — only now do we need how \(W\) changes with \(\phi\). So at this moment we compute \(\dfrac{dW}{d\phi}\): \(W=(1-e^2\sin^2\phi)^{1/2}\), chain rule; first the innermost \(\dfrac{d}{d\phi}\sin^2\phi=2\sin\phi\cos\phi\), then work outward:

\[ \frac{dW}{d\phi} =\tfrac12\bigl(1-e^2\sin^2\phi\bigr)^{-1/2}\cdot\frac{d}{d\phi}\bigl(1-e^2\sin^2\phi\bigr) =\tfrac12\,W^{-1}\cdot\bigl(-e^2\cdot 2\sin\phi\cos\phi\bigr) =-\frac{e^2\sin\phi\cos\phi}{W} \] \[ \Rightarrow\quad \bigl(W^{-1}\bigr)'=-W^{-2}\frac{dW}{d\phi} =-W^{-2}\Bigl(-\frac{e^2\sin\phi\cos\phi}{W}\Bigr) =\frac{e^2\sin\phi\cos\phi}{W^3} \]

（两个负号相乘变正，\(W^{-2}\cdot W^{-1}=W^{-3}\)。）把这块代回上面停住的地方，再通分到 \(W^3\)、提公因子，最后把 \(W^2=1-e^2\sin^2\phi\) 展开收拢——简化是自己冒出来的，不是凑的：(Two minus signs make a plus; \(W^{-2}\cdot W^{-1}=W^{-3}\).) Put this back where we paused, then common-denominator \(W^3\), factor, and finally expand \(W^2=1-e^2\sin^2\phi\) and collect — the simplification emerges, it is not forced:

\[ \frac{dp}{d\phi}=-a\sin\phi\cdot W^{-1}+a\cos\phi\cdot\frac{e^2\sin\phi\cos\phi}{W^3} =-\frac{a\sin\phi}{W}+\frac{a\,e^2\sin\phi\cos^2\phi}{W^3} \] \[ \overset{\text{common denom. }W^3}{=}\; \frac{-a\sin\phi\,W^2+a\,e^2\sin\phi\cos^2\phi}{W^3} =\frac{a\sin\phi}{W^3}\bigl(-W^2+e^2\cos^2\phi\bigr) \] \[ -W^2+e^2\cos^2\phi =-(1-e^2\sin^2\phi)+e^2\cos^2\phi =-1+e^2\underbrace{(\sin^2\phi+\cos^2\phi)}_{=\,1} =-(1-e^2) \] \[ \Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dp}{d\phi}=\frac{a\sin\phi}{W^3}\cdot\bigl(-(1-e^2)\bigr)=-\frac{a(1-e^2)\sin\phi}{W^3} \]

3.4 求 \(\dfrac{dz}{d\phi}\)（\(\dfrac{dW}{d\phi}\) 已经有了，直接用）3.4 Compute \(\dfrac{dz}{d\phi}\) (\(\dfrac{dW}{d\phi}\) is already in hand — reuse it)

同样的乘积法则，\(z=a(1-e^2)\sin\phi\cdot W^{-1}\)，常数 \(a(1-e^2)\) 提前，取 \(u=\sin\phi\)（\(u'=\cos\phi\)）、\(v=W^{-1}\)；这次 \(\bigl(W^{-1}\bigr)'\) 不用再算，就是 3.3 刚得到的 \(\dfrac{e^2\sin\phi\cos\phi}{W^3}\)：Same product rule: \(z=a(1-e^2)\sin\phi\cdot W^{-1}\), constant \(a(1-e^2)\) out front, \(u=\sin\phi\) (\(u'=\cos\phi\)), \(v=W^{-1}\); this time \(\bigl(W^{-1}\bigr)'\) needs no recomputing — it is 3.3’s \(\dfrac{e^2\sin\phi\cos\phi}{W^3}\):

\[ \frac{dz}{d\phi}=a(1-e^2)\bigl[u'v+uv'\bigr] =a(1-e^2)\Bigl[\cos\phi\cdot W^{-1}+\sin\phi\cdot\frac{e^2\sin\phi\cos\phi}{W^3}\Bigr] \] \[ =a(1-e^2)\Bigl[\frac{\cos\phi}{W}+\frac{e^2\sin^2\phi\cos\phi}{W^3}\Bigr] \overset{\text{common denom. }W^3}{=}\; \frac{a(1-e^2)\cos\phi}{W^3}\bigl(W^2+e^2\sin^2\phi\bigr) \] \[ W^2+e^2\sin^2\phi=(1-e^2\sin^2\phi)+e^2\sin^2\phi=1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dz}{d\phi}=\frac{a(1-e^2)\cos\phi}{W^3} \]

3.5 代回 3.2 的目标式 → \(R_M\)（每步写全）3.5 Plug back into 3.2’s goal → \(R_M\) (every step)

两个导数都有了，代进 3.2 的 \(\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}=\sqrt{\bigl(\tfrac{dp}{d\phi}\bigr)^2+\bigl(\tfrac{dz}{d\phi}\bigr)^2}\)。先分别平方（注意 \(\bigl(\tfrac{1}{W^3}\bigr)^2=\tfrac{1}{W^6}\)）：Both derivatives are in hand; plug into 3.2’s \(\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}=\sqrt{\bigl(\tfrac{dp}{d\phi}\bigr)^2+\bigl(\tfrac{dz}{d\phi}\bigr)^2}\). Square each first (note \(\bigl(\tfrac{1}{W^3}\bigr)^2=\tfrac{1}{W^6}\)):

\[ \Bigl(\frac{dp}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2=\frac{a^2(1-e^2)^2\sin^2\phi}{W^6}, \qquad \Bigl(\frac{dz}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2=\frac{a^2(1-e^2)^2\cos^2\phi}{W^6} \] \[ \Bigl(\frac{dp}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2+\Bigl(\frac{dz}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2 =\frac{a^2(1-e^2)^2}{W^6}\,\underbrace{\bigl(\sin^2\phi+\cos^2\phi\bigr)}_{=\,1} =\frac{a^2(1-e^2)^2}{W^6} \] \[ \frac{ds}{d\phi}=\sqrt{\Bigl(\frac{dp}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2+\Bigl(\frac{dz}{d\phi}\Bigr)^2} =\sqrt{\frac{a^2(1-e^2)^2}{W^6}}=\frac{a(1-e^2)}{W^3} \quad(a,\,1-e^2,\,W^3>0) \] \[ \boxed{\;R_M=\frac{ds}{d\phi}=\frac{a(1-e^2)}{W^3}=\frac{a(1-e^2)}{\bigl(1-e^2\sin^2\phi\bigr)^{3/2}}\;} \]
两个漂亮的副产品。 (1) 子午线切线方向 \(\bigl(\tfrac{dp}{d\phi},\tfrac{dz}{d\phi}\bigr)\propto(-\sin\phi,\cos\phi)\)，正好垂直于第 2 步的法线 \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\)——前后自洽。 (2) \(R_N\ge R_M\) 可以直接证：\(\dfrac{R_M}{R_N}=\dfrac{a(1-e^2)/W^3}{a/W}=\dfrac{1-e^2}{W^2}=\dfrac{1-e^2}{1-e^2\sin^2\phi}\le1\)（因 \(\sin^2\phi\le1\)）；赤道处比值最小 \(=1-e^2\)，两极处 \(R_M=R_N=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{1-e^2}}\)。 Two nice by-products. (1) The meridian tangent direction \(\bigl(\tfrac{dp}{d\phi},\tfrac{dz}{d\phi}\bigr)\propto(-\sin\phi,\cos\phi)\) is exactly perpendicular to Step 2’s normal \((\cos\phi,\sin\phi)\) — fully consistent. (2) \(R_N\ge R_M\) drops right out: \(\dfrac{R_M}{R_N}=\dfrac{1-e^2}{W^2}=\dfrac{1-e^2}{1-e^2\sin^2\phi}\le1\) (since \(\sin^2\phi\le1\)); the ratio is smallest at the equator \((=1-e^2)\) and \(R_M=R_N=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{1-e^2}}\) at the poles.
数值自检：上面每一步导数都和数值微分逐点吻合（如 \(\phi=45^\circ\)：\(\tfrac{dp}{d\phi}=\tfrac{dz}{d\phi}\) 同量级、\(R_M=6367.382\) km）；得到的 \(R_M\) 公式与代码里 R_M = WGS84_A*(1.0-WGS84_E2)/denom**1.5（\(\texttt{denom}=W^2\)）逐位一致。 Numerical self-check: every derivative above matches finite differences pointwise (e.g. at \(\phi=45^\circ\): \(\tfrac{dp}{d\phi}=\tfrac{dz}{d\phi}\) in magnitude, \(R_M=6367.382\) km); the resulting \(R_M\) formula matches the code’s R_M = WGS84_A*(1.0-WGS84_E2)/denom**1.5 (\(\texttt{denom}=W^2\)) to the last digit.

第 4 步：两个半径的公式（小结）Step 4: The Two Radius Formulas (summary)

前面已经分别推出两个半径——\(R_N=a/W\)（第 2 步）和 \(R_M=a(1-e^2)/W^3\)（第 3 步）。这里把它们并排小结：形状一模一样，同一个 \(W\)，只是幂不同（1 次方 vs 3 次方）： Both radii are now derived — \(R_N=a/W\) (Step 2) and \(R_M=a(1-e^2)/W^3\) (Step 3). Here they are side by side: identical shape, the same \(W\), only a different power (1st vs 3rd):

\[ R_N=\frac{a}{W}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\phi}} \] \[ R_M=\frac{a(1-e^2)}{W^3}=\frac{a(1-e^2)}{\left(1-e^2\sin^2\phi\right)^{3/2}} \]

这正是代码里的两行（注意 denom \(=W^2\)）： These are exactly the two code lines (note denom \(=W^2\)): 

# make_geodetic_dynamics_model()
denom = 1.0 - WGS84_E2 * sin(lat)**2     # = W^2
R_N = WGS84_A / sqrt(denom)            # prime-vertical
R_M = WGS84_A * (1.0 - WGS84_E2) / denom**1.5  # meridional

几何含义：两个密切圆Geometric Meaning: Two Osculating Circles

在子午剖面里，过某点 P 沿法线方向有两条“最贴合”的圆弧。\(R_M\) 是沿子午线（南北）走时贴合的密切圆半径；\(R_N\) 是法线一直延伸到自转轴的长度，决定东西方向的弯曲。在 WGS 84 上总有 \(R_N\ge R_M\)，且二者都随纬度变化——拖动滑块感受一下。 In the meridian section, two “best-fitting” circular arcs pass through a point P along the normal. \(R_M\) is the osculating-circle radius when moving along the meridian (north–south); \(R_N\) is the length of the normal extended to the spin axis, controlling east–west bending. On WGS 84 always \(R_N\ge R_M\), and both vary with latitude — drag the slider to feel it.

赤道处 \(R_M\) 最小、两极处最大；\(R_N\) 反之在两极最大。差别不大（千分之几），但在大地动力学里它必须被正确区分，否则南北/东西换算就会系统性偏一点点。 \(R_M\) is smallest at the equator and largest at the poles; \(R_N\) is largest at the poles too. The difference is small (a few parts per thousand) but must be kept distinct in geodetic dynamics, or the north–south versus east–west conversions pick up a small systematic bias.

6. 坐标变换 A：位置运动学，米/秒 → 经纬度弧度/秒Transformation A: Position Kinematics, Metres/Second → Radians/Second

这是第一处真正的变换。平面模型写 \(\dot x=V_E,\ \dot y=V_N\)（米/秒）。大地模型必须把同样的线速度换成纬度、经度的角速度。换算因子就是上一节的曲率半径。 This is the first real transformation. The flat model writes \(\dot x=V_E,\ \dot y=V_N\) (metres/second). The geodetic model must turn the same linear velocities into angular rates of latitude and longitude. The conversion factors are the curvature radii from the previous section.

先想清楚“为什么是这两个半径”，再写公式。 关键问题不是“\(R_M,R_N\) 怎么算”（第 5 节已解决），而是“凭什么用它们换经纬度”。下面三步把这个逻辑补全——你会看到 \(R_M\) 和 \(R_N\) 进公式靠的是两条完全不同的理由。 First settle “why these two radii,” then write the formula. The real question is not “how to compute \(R_M,R_N\)” (Section 5 did that) but “what entitles us to use them to convert to lat/lon.” The three steps below fill in that logic — and you’ll see \(R_M\) and \(R_N\) enter for two entirely different reasons.
北向 → 纬度率（用 \(R_M\)，理由 = 它的定义）。向北 = 沿子午线走。第 5 节第 3 步已经证明 \(R_M=\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}\)——子午线上每 1 弧度纬度对应 \(R_M\) 米弧长。反过来：走 \(\delta N\) 米就转过 \(\delta\phi=\delta N/R_M\)；在高度 \(h\) 处本地子午线半径放大成 \(R_M+h\)。所以 North → latitude rate (use \(R_M\); the reason is its very definition). North = along the meridian. Section 5 Step 3 proved \(R_M=\dfrac{ds}{d\phi}\) — one radian of latitude = \(R_M\) metres of meridian arc. Invert it: \(\delta N\) metres turns \(\delta\phi=\delta N/R_M\); at height \(h\) the local meridian radius grows to \(R_M+h\). Hence
\[\dot\phi=\frac{V_N}{R_M+h}\]
这就是 \(R_M\) 能算纬度的全部理由：它的定义本身就是“弧长 ÷ 纬度角”。That is the whole reason \(R_M\) computes latitude: it is defined as “arc length ÷ latitude angle.”
东向 → 经度率（用 \(R_N\)，理由完全不同）。向东 = 沿纬线圈（同一纬度的圆）走。一个半径为 \(p\) 的圆，走弧长 \(\delta E\) 转过的圆心角是 \(\delta\lambda=\delta E/p\)。于是只剩一个问题：纬线圈半径 \(p\)（= P 到自转轴的垂直距离）等于多少？ East → longitude rate (use \(R_N\); for a totally different reason). East = along the parallel (the circle of constant latitude). On a circle of radius \(p\), travelling arc \(\delta E\) sweeps a central angle \(\delta\lambda=\delta E/p\). So the only question left: what is the parallel radius \(p\) (the perpendicular distance from P to the spin axis)?

答案第 5 节第 2 步早就算出来了：地表点 \(P\) 的水平坐标 \(x_0=N\cos\phi\)，其中 \(N=R_N=a/W\)（法线到自转轴的长度）。所以地表纬线圈半径 \(p_0=R_N\cos\phi\)。再加高度 \(h\)（沿法线 \(\hat{\mathbf n}\) 向外，\(\hat{\mathbf n}\) 的水平分量是 \(\cos\phi\)，多出 \(h\cos\phi\)）： The answer was already computed in Section 5 Step 2: the surface point \(P\)’s horizontal coordinate is \(x_0=N\cos\phi\), where \(N=R_N=a/W\) (the normal’s length to the spin axis). So the surface parallel radius is \(p_0=R_N\cos\phi\). Add height \(h\) (outward along \(\hat{\mathbf n}\), whose horizontal part is \(\cos\phi\), adding \(h\cos\phi\)):

\[ p=(R_N+h)\cos\phi \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \dot\lambda=\frac{V_E}{p}=\frac{V_E}{(R_N+h)\cos\phi} \]
（下图把这条逻辑画出来。）(The figure below draws this logic.)
天向 → 高度率。沿法线向上的速度直接就是高度率，没有曲率换算： Up → height rate. The upward velocity along the normal is the height rate directly — no curvature conversion:
\[\dot h=V_U\]
两个半径，两条不同的逻辑（这就是你问的“为什么”）。 \(R_M\) 直接当“弧长 ÷ 纬度角”用——这是它的定义（第 3 步）。\(R_N\) 是间接的：真正进经度公式的是纬线圈半径 \((R_N+h)\cos\phi\)；\(R_N\) 之所以出现，纯粹是因为第 2 步证明了 \(R_N\cos\phi\) 恰好等于“到自转轴的水平距离”——和它叫不叫“曲率半径”毫无关系。 Two radii, two different logics (this is the “why” you asked about). \(R_M\) is used directly as “arc length ÷ latitude angle” — that is its definition (Step 3). \(R_N\) is indirect: what actually enters the longitude formula is the parallel radius \((R_N+h)\cos\phi\); \(R_N\) appears only because Step 2 proved \(R_N\cos\phi\) equals the horizontal distance to the spin axis — nothing to do with it being called a “radius of curvature.”
更深一层（可跳过）：\(R_N\) 确实也是“卯酉法截面”（含法线、朝东西的竖直切面）的曲率半径；而纬线圈是同样朝东西、但相对法线倾斜了 \(\phi\) 的斜截面，按 Meusnier 定理它的半径 \(=R_N\cos\phi\)——和上面的结论一致。但要算经度位置率，只需要“纬线圈半径”这一步，不必动用曲率。 One layer deeper (skippable): \(R_N\) is also the radius of curvature of the “prime-vertical normal section” (the east–west vertical slice through the normal); the parallel is the same east–west direction but a section tilted by \(\phi\) from the normal, so by Meusnier’s theorem its radius \(=R_N\cos\phi\) — consistent with the above. But for the longitude position rate you only need the “parallel radius” step, no curvature required.

7. 中间桥：逐步重锚 ENU 步进器The Bridge: The Per-Step Re-anchored ENU Stepper

在写出连续大地 RHS 之前，项目先有一个“离散但精确”的中间方案：make_geo_step_from_enu_integrator。它的想法极简单——既然平面只在原点附近准，那就每一步都把原点搬到飞机当前位置，用全新的切平面走一步，再转回大地坐标。 Before writing the continuous geodetic RHS, the project first had a “discrete but accurate” middle option: make_geo_step_from_enu_integrator. The idea is simple — since the plane is only accurate near its origin, move the origin to the aircraft’s current position every step, take one step in a brand-new tangent plane, then convert back to geodetic.

一步映射 \(\Phi\) 的内部Inside the One-Step Map \(\Phi\)

每个 RK4 步做四件事：Each RK4 step does four things:

  1. 在当前 \((\phi,\lambda)\) 处、以 \(h\) 为竖直起点，建一块新切平面，把当前状态写成 ENU 原点处的 \((0,0,h,V,\psi,\gamma)\)。Build a fresh tangent plane at the current \((\phi,\lambda)\), and write the current state at the ENU origin as \((0,0,h,V,\psi,\gamma)\).
  2. 用平面 RHS（make_dynamics_model）走一个 RK4 步，得到 ENU 里的下一个状态。Take one RK4 step with the flat RHS (make_dynamics_model) to get the next ENU state.
  3. 把新的 ENU 位置 + 速度转回大地坐标（enu_state_to_geodetic_expr）。Convert the new ENU position and velocity back to geodetic (enu_state_to_geodetic_expr).
  4. 丢弃这块切平面；下一步重新来过。Discard the tangent plane; start over next step.
\[ \mathbf x_{k+1}=\Phi(\mathbf x_k,\mathbf u_k,\Delta t) \]

为什么它“离散”很关键Why “Discrete” Matters

\(\Phi\) 是一个一步映射，不是连续右端 \(f\)。这带来一个实际限制：直接配点里的多项式方案（梯形、Hermite-Simpson、RK4 缺陷）需要对连续 \(f\) 求值并拟合多项式，喂不进一个步进器。步进器只能当“打靶”缺陷用：\(\mathbf x_{k+1}-\Phi(\mathbf x_k,\mathbf u_k,\Delta t)=0\)。 \(\Phi\) is a one-step map, not a continuous right-hand side \(f\). That brings a practical limit: polynomial collocation schemes (trapezoidal, Hermite-Simpson, RK4 defect) evaluate and fit a polynomial to a continuous \(f\), so a stepper cannot feed them. A stepper can only serve as a shooting defect: \(\mathbf x_{k+1}-\Phi(\mathbf x_k,\mathbf u_k,\Delta t)=0\).

这正是要走到“终点”的动机。 重锚步进器精确，但离散。要在最优化里用多项式配点，需要一条连续的大地 RHS——它在每个点上的瞬时行为，等于“无穷小重锚”的极限。第 8 节先备齐这个极限的另一半（输运率），第 9 节再把整条连续 RHS 写出来、并证明它正是这个极限。 This is precisely the motivation for the “end.” The re-anchored stepper is accurate but discrete. To use polynomial collocation in optimization, we need a continuous geodetic RHS whose instantaneous behaviour at each point equals the limit of “infinitesimal re-anchoring.” Section 8 first supplies the other half of that limit (the transport rate), and Section 9 writes the full continuous RHS and proves it is exactly this limit.

为什么步进器要绕 ENU→ECEF→大地 一大圈，而连续方案“除以半径”就够了？Why the Stepper Round-Trips ENU→ECEF→Geodetic, While the Continuous Scheme Just “Divides by Radius”

因为步进器复用平直 RHS（make_dynamics_model），而那套方程只认平直 ENU 的米。它没法直接吃一个大地坐标状态，于是每一步都被迫绕一圈： Because the stepper reuses the flat RHS (make_dynamics_model), and those equations only understand flat ENU metres. They cannot be evaluated on a geodetic state directly, so every step is forced through a round-trip:

\[ \underbrace{(\phi,\lambda,h)}_{\text{geodetic, deg}} \;\xrightarrow{\;\text{geo}\to\text{ECEF}\to\text{ENU}\;}\; \underbrace{(0,0,h,V,\psi,\gamma)}_{\text{flat ENU, m}} \;\xrightarrow{\;\text{flat RHS, one RK4 step}\;}\; (\delta E,\delta N,\delta U,\dots) \;\xrightarrow{\;\text{ENU}\to\text{ECEF}\to\text{geo}\;}\; (\phi',\lambda',h') \]

这趟来回是精确的，但它是“复用平直模型”要付的代价：每一步都数值地做一次坐标换算。连续大地 RHS 不复用平直方程，而是直接把动力学写在大地坐标里——它把这趟换算替换成换算的导数（雅可比）。对位置而言，这个雅可比恰好是一个对角的“除以半径”： That round-trip is exact, but it is the price of reusing the flat model: a numerical coordinate conversion every step. The continuous geodetic RHS does not reuse the flat equations; it writes the dynamics directly in geodetic coordinates — replacing the conversion by its derivative (Jacobian). For position, that Jacobian is exactly a diagonal “divide by radius”:

\[ \delta N=(R_M+h)\,\delta\phi,\qquad \delta E=(R_N+h)\cos\phi\,\delta\lambda,\qquad \delta U=\delta h \]

“沿子午线走 \(\delta N\) 米，纬度增加 \(\delta N/(R_M+h)\)；沿纬线（半径 \((R_N+h)\cos\phi\)）走 \(\delta E\) 米，经度增加 \(\delta E/((R_N+h)\cos\phi)\)。” 这里的 \(R_M,R_N\) 正是图 2b 的两个曲率半径。把它写成雅可比： “Walk \(\delta N\) metres along the meridian → latitude rises by \(\delta N/(R_M+h)\); walk \(\delta E\) metres along the parallel (radius \((R_N+h)\cos\phi\)) → longitude rises by \(\delta E/((R_N+h)\cos\phi)\).” The \(R_M,R_N\) here are exactly Figure 2b’s two radii of curvature. As a Jacobian:

\[ J=\left.\frac{\partial(\phi,\lambda,h)}{\partial(E,N,U)}\right|_{0} =\begin{pmatrix} 0 & \dfrac{1}{R_M+h} & 0\\[8pt] \dfrac{1}{(R_N+h)\cos\phi} & 0 & 0\\[8pt] 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \]
关键区别：“除以半径”只是换算的微分（瞬时率）。你不能用它替换一次有限的 ENU→大地换算——那只是某一点的线性化，走远了就偏。所以有限步长的步进器仍得做完整的非线性来回；而连续 RHS 是瞬时的，用线性雅可比正好。两者是不同的离散算子，但它们有相同的连续极限（第 9 节给出证明）。 The key distinction: “divide by radius” is only the differential (instantaneous rate) of the conversion. You cannot use it to replace one finite ENU→geodetic conversion — that is just the linearization at a point and drifts over distance. So the finite-step stepper still does the full nonlinear round-trip, while the continuous RHS, being instantaneous, uses the linear Jacobian. They are different discrete operators — but with the same continuous limit (proven in Section 9).

8. 坐标变换 B：输运率（这页最微妙的一处）Transformation B: The Transport Rate (the subtlest part of this page)

\(\psi\) 和 \(\gamma\) 不是相对惯性空间定义的，而是相对当地 ENU 框架。可这个框架本身随飞机在曲面上移动而转动（图 3 看得很清楚）。于是即使气动力矩为零，\(\psi,\gamma\) 也会变。这个“纯几何造成的角速度”叫输运率。连续大地 RHS 必须把它显式补上，才能等于重锚步进器。 \(\psi\) and \(\gamma\) are defined not against inertial space but against the local ENU frame. Yet that frame itself rotates as the aircraft moves over the curved surface (clearly visible in Figure 3). So even with zero aerodynamic moment, \(\psi,\gamma\) change. This “purely geometric angular velocity” is the transport rate. The continuous geodetic RHS must add it explicitly to equal the re-anchored stepper.

本节路线（五步，不跳步）： ① 把 \(\psi,\gamma\) 写成"速度方向在当地 ENU 里的两个角"；② 求当地框架的转动角速度 \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\)；③ 用输运定理把"力造成的转动"和"我们读到的角速率"分开；④ 把方向变化率投影回 \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\)；⑤ 代入、取主项，得到代码里的两行。 Roadmap (five steps, none skipped): ① write \(\psi,\gamma\) as two angles of the velocity direction in the local ENU frame; ② find the frame’s angular velocity \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\); ③ use the transport theorem to separate "rotation from forces" from "the rate we read off"; ④ project the direction-rate back onto \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\); ⑤ substitute and keep the leading terms to land on the two code lines.

第 1 步：\(\psi,\gamma\) 是"速度方向"在当地 ENU 里的两个角Step 1: \(\psi,\gamma\) Are Two Angles of the Velocity Direction in the Local ENU Frame

把单位速度向量 \(\hat{\mathbf v}\)（速度除以速率 \(V\)）写在 East-North-Up 三轴上。航向 \(\psi\) 是它在水平面内相对东轴的角，航迹角 \(\gamma\) 是它相对水平面的仰角： Write the unit velocity vector \(\hat{\mathbf v}\) (velocity over speed \(V\)) on the East-North-Up axes. Heading \(\psi\) is its angle from East in the horizontal plane; flight-path angle \(\gamma\) is its elevation above the horizontal:

\[ \hat{\mathbf v}=\frac{1}{V}\begin{pmatrix}V_E\\V_N\\V_U\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix}\cos\gamma\cos\psi\\[2pt]\cos\gamma\sin\psi\\[2pt]\sin\gamma\end{pmatrix}_{ENU} \]

关键：这两个角是相对 ENU 这组轴量的。而 ENU 轴本身钉在地表，随飞机移动会转。所以哪怕 \(\hat{\mathbf v}\) 在惯性空间里完全不动，只要框架转了，读出来的 \(\psi,\gamma\) 也会变。这部分变化就是输运率，下面四步把它精确算出来。 The key point: these two angles are measured against the ENU axes, and those axes are pinned to the surface and rotate as the aircraft moves. So even if \(\hat{\mathbf v}\) were frozen in inertial space, the read-off \(\psi,\gamma\) would still change once the frame turns. That change is the transport rate, computed exactly in the next four steps.

第 2 步：求当地框架的转动角速度 \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\)Step 2: Find the Local Frame’s Angular Velocity \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\)

ENU 轴的朝向只由 \((\phi,\lambda)\) 决定。飞机一动，\(\phi,\lambda\) 变，轴就转。转动只来自两个独立部分： The ENU axes’ orientation depends only on \((\phi,\lambda)\). When the aircraft moves, \(\phi,\lambda\) change and the axes rotate. The rotation comes from just two independent pieces:

  • 经度变化 \(\dot\lambda\)：是绕地球自转轴 \(\hat{\mathbf z}_e\) 的转动。把自转轴写到 ENU 三轴上（它在 East 上投影为 0、North 上为 \(\cos\phi\)、Up 上为 \(\sin\phi\)）：\(\hat{\mathbf z}_e=\cos\phi\,\hat{\mathbf n}+\sin\phi\,\hat{\mathbf u}\)。Longitude change \(\dot\lambda\) is a rotation about the Earth’s spin axis \(\hat{\mathbf z}_e\). On the ENU axes (its East component is 0, North \(\cos\phi\), Up \(\sin\phi\)): \(\hat{\mathbf z}_e=\cos\phi\,\hat{\mathbf n}+\sin\phi\,\hat{\mathbf u}\).
  • 纬度变化 \(\dot\phi\)：向北走时整个框架绕东轴倒下，转轴是 \(-\hat{\mathbf e}\)。（验证：\(\partial\hat{\mathbf u}/\partial\phi=\hat{\mathbf n}\)、\(\partial\hat{\mathbf n}/\partial\phi=-\hat{\mathbf u}\)，正是绕 \(-\hat{\mathbf e}\) 的旋转。）Latitude change \(\dot\phi\): moving north tips the whole frame about the East axis, with rotation axis \(-\hat{\mathbf e}\). (Check: \(\partial\hat{\mathbf u}/\partial\phi=\hat{\mathbf n}\), \(\partial\hat{\mathbf n}/\partial\phi=-\hat{\mathbf u}\) — exactly a rotation about \(-\hat{\mathbf e}\).)

把两部分相加，再换到 ENU 分量：Add the two pieces, then read the ENU components:

\[ \boldsymbol\omega_{en}=\dot\lambda\,\hat{\mathbf z}_e-\dot\phi\,\hat{\mathbf e} =-\dot\phi\,\hat{\mathbf e}+\dot\lambda\cos\phi\,\hat{\mathbf n}+\dot\lambda\sin\phi\,\hat{\mathbf u} \] \[ \boldsymbol\omega_{en}^{ENU}=(\omega_E,\ \omega_N,\ \omega_U) =\bigl(-\dot\phi,\ \dot\lambda\cos\phi,\ \dot\lambda\sin\phi\bigr) \]

代入第 6 节的位置率 \(\dot\phi=\dfrac{V_N}{R_M+h}\)、\(\dot\lambda=\dfrac{V_E}{(R_N+h)\cos\phi}\)（注意第三分量里 \(\dot\lambda\sin\phi\) 的 \(\cos\phi\) 被约掉变成 \(\tan\phi\)）：Substitute the position rates from Section 6, \(\dot\phi=\dfrac{V_N}{R_M+h}\), \(\dot\lambda=\dfrac{V_E}{(R_N+h)\cos\phi}\) (in the third component the \(\cos\phi\) cancels, leaving \(\tan\phi\)):

\[ \boldsymbol\omega_{en}^{ENU}= \left(\; \underbrace{-\frac{V_N}{R_M+h}}_{\omega_E},\quad \underbrace{\frac{V_E}{R_N+h}}_{\omega_N},\quad \underbrace{\frac{V_E\tan\phi}{R_N+h}}_{\omega_U} \;\right) \]

第 3 步：输运定理——分开"力造成的转动"和"读到的角速率"Step 3: The Transport Theorem — Separate "Rotation from Forces" from "the Rate We Read"

对任意向量，惯性系里的变化率 = 转动系里的变化率 + 框架自转贡献（输运定理）。本模型忽略地球自转，所以"惯性系"≈地球系 \(e\)；"转动系"是当地 ENU 系 \(n\)： For any vector, the rate in the inertial frame equals the rate in the rotating frame plus the frame’s own spin (the transport theorem). This model ignores Earth’s rotation, so "inertial" ≈ Earth frame \(e\); the "rotating frame" is the local ENU frame \(n\):

\[ \left.\frac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}\right|_{e} =\left.\frac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}\right|_{n} +\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v} \]

牛顿定律在 \(e\) 系成立，所以气动力 + 重力给出的是左边 \(\big(d\hat{\mathbf v}/dt\big)|_{e}\)。而平直模型的那两条姿态方程（第 3 节的 \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\)）是假设框架不转推出来的，等于把力造成的转动直接当成了 \(n\) 系里的角速率。真正在 \(n\) 系里读到的是右边第一项，于是要补的修正就是 \(-\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v}\)： Newton’s law holds in \(e\), so aerodynamic force + gravity give the left side \(\big(d\hat{\mathbf v}/dt\big)|_{e}\). The flat model’s two attitude equations (the \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\) of Section 3) were derived assuming the frame does not rotate — they equate the force-induced turn directly with the \(n\)-frame angular rates. What we actually read in \(n\) is the first right-hand term, so the correction to add is \(-\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v}\):

\[ \underbrace{\left.\frac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}\right|_{n}}_{\text{measured }\dot\psi,\dot\gamma} =\underbrace{\left.\frac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}\right|_{e}}_{\text{aero, Step 3}} \;\underbrace{-\;\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v}}_{\text{transport correction}} \]

第 4 步：把"方向变化率"投影回 \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\)Step 4: Project the "Direction Rate" Back onto \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\)

\(\hat{\mathbf v}\) 只有两个自由度 \((\psi,\gamma)\)。对它们求偏导，得到两条互相垂直的单位方向——\(\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}\)（仰角增大的方向）和 \(\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}\)（航向增大的方向）： \(\hat{\mathbf v}\) has only two degrees of freedom \((\psi,\gamma)\). Differentiating with respect to them gives two mutually perpendicular unit directions — \(\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}\) (increasing elevation) and \(\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}\) (increasing heading):

\[ \hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}=\frac{\partial\hat{\mathbf v}}{\partial\gamma} =\begin{pmatrix}-\sin\gamma\cos\psi\\-\sin\gamma\sin\psi\\\cos\gamma\end{pmatrix}, \qquad \frac{\partial\hat{\mathbf v}}{\partial\psi}=\cos\gamma\,\hat{\boldsymbol\psi},\quad \hat{\boldsymbol\psi}=\begin{pmatrix}-\sin\psi\\\cos\psi\\0\end{pmatrix} \]

于是任意方向变化率 \(\dfrac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}=\dot\gamma\,\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}+\dot\psi\cos\gamma\,\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}\)，反解出"读数规则"——把变化率点到两条单位方向上： So any direction rate is \(\dfrac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}=\dot\gamma\,\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}+\dot\psi\cos\gamma\,\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}\); inverting gives the "read-off rule" — dot the rate onto the two unit directions:

\[ \dot\gamma=\frac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}, \qquad \dot\psi=\frac{1}{\cos\gamma}\,\frac{d\hat{\mathbf v}}{dt}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\psi} \]

第 5 步：代入修正项，取主项 → 对上代码Step 5: Substitute the Correction, Keep the Leading Terms → Match the Code

把第 4 步的读数规则作用在第 3 步的修正 \(-\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v}\) 上。用标量三重积 \((\mathbf A\times\mathbf B)\cdot\mathbf C=\mathbf A\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf C)\) 与两条恒等式 \(\hat{\mathbf v}\times\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}=-\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}\)、\(\hat{\mathbf v}\times\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}=\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}\)，叉积一下就消掉： Apply Step 4’s rule to Step 3’s correction \(-\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v}\). Using the scalar triple product \((\mathbf A\times\mathbf B)\cdot\mathbf C=\mathbf A\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf C)\) and the two identities \(\hat{\mathbf v}\times\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}=-\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}\), \(\hat{\mathbf v}\times\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}=\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}\), the cross products collapse:

\[ \Delta\dot\gamma=(-\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v})\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma} =\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}, \qquad \Delta\dot\psi=\frac{(-\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\times\hat{\mathbf v})\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}}{\cos\gamma} =-\frac{\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}}{\cos\gamma} \]

航迹角 \(\gamma\)（结果是严格的）：点积 \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}=-\omega_E\sin\psi+\omega_N\cos\psi\)，再用 \(V_N=V\cos\gamma\sin\psi\)、\(V_E=V\cos\gamma\cos\psi\)：Flight-path angle \(\gamma\) (this result is exact): the dot product \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\psi}=-\omega_E\sin\psi+\omega_N\cos\psi\), then with \(V_N=V\cos\gamma\sin\psi\), \(V_E=V\cos\gamma\cos\psi\):

\[ \Delta\dot\gamma=\frac{V_N\sin\psi}{R_M+h}+\frac{V_E\cos\psi}{R_N+h} =V\cos\gamma\left(\frac{\sin^2\psi}{R_M+h}+\frac{\cos^2\psi}{R_N+h}\right) \]

航向 \(\psi\)（保留主项）：展开 \(-\dfrac{\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}}{\cos\gamma}\) 得到两项——主项是子午线收敛 \(-\omega_U/\!\cos\gamma\cdot\cos\gamma\)，外加一个小交叉项：Heading \(\psi\) (keep the leading term): expanding \(-\dfrac{\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol\gamma}}{\cos\gamma}\) gives two pieces — the dominant meridian-convergence term plus a small cross term:

\[ \Delta\dot\psi= \underbrace{-\frac{V\cos\gamma\cos\psi\tan\phi}{R_N+h}}_{\text{kept: meridian convergence}} +\underbrace{V\sin\gamma\sin\psi\cos\psi\!\left(\frac{1}{R_N+h}-\frac{1}{R_M+h}\right)}_{\text{dropped}} \]

物理上看：航向 \(\psi\) 主要被框架的竖直自转分量 \(\omega_U\)（\(=\) 子午线收敛）转动，这就是保留项；水平分量 \(\omega_E,\omega_N\) 只通过 \(\tan\gamma\) 的倾斜耦合泄漏进 \(\psi\)，正是被丢弃的那块。它同时含 \(\sin\gamma\)（在水平飞行 \(\gamma=0\) 时恰好为零，否则也很小）和 \(\bigl(\tfrac{1}{R_N+h}-\tfrac{1}{R_M+h}\bigr)\)（因 \(R_N\approx R_M\)，差仅约 \(e^2\) 量级）——两个小量相乘，实测比保留项小约 \(10^{4}\) 倍（\(\gamma=-10^\circ\) 也只占 \(0.02\%\)），故略去。注意这是仅 \(\dot\psi\) 的近似：\(\dot\gamma\) 那项是严格的、不丢任何东西。得到代码里的两行： Physically: heading \(\psi\) is rotated mainly by the frame’s vertical spin component \(\omega_U\) (\(=\) meridian convergence) — that is the kept term; the horizontal components \(\omega_E,\omega_N\) leak into \(\psi\) only through the \(\tan\gamma\) tilt coupling, which is exactly the dropped piece. It carries \(\sin\gamma\) (exactly zero in level flight \(\gamma=0\), otherwise small) and \(\bigl(\tfrac{1}{R_N+h}-\tfrac{1}{R_M+h}\bigr)\) (since \(R_N\approx R_M\), of order \(e^2\)) — two small factors multiplied, numerically \(\sim10^{4}\times\) smaller than the kept term (only \(0.02\%\) even at \(\gamma=-10^\circ\)), so it is dropped. Note this approximation is for \(\dot\psi\) only: the \(\dot\gamma\) term is exact, nothing dropped. This yields the two code lines:

\[ \dot\gamma \mathrel{+}= V\cos\gamma\left( \frac{\sin^2\psi}{R_M+h}+\frac{\cos^2\psi}{R_N+h} \right) \qquad\text{(curvature pitch-up)} \] \[ \dot\psi \mathrel{+}= -\,\frac{V\cos\gamma\,\cos\psi\,\tan\phi}{R_N+h} \qquad\text{(meridian convergence)} \]
数值自检（某状态 \(V=130,\ \psi=34^\circ,\ \gamma=-2.9^\circ,\ \phi=42^\circ,\ h=3\,\mathrm{km}\)）：严格投影与上面两行代码逐项吻合，\(\dot\gamma\) 完全相等，\(\dot\psi\) 仅差被丢弃的交叉项 \(\sim1.8\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{rad/s}\)（占保留项的 \(0.01\%\)）。 Numerical self-check (state \(V=130,\ \psi=34^\circ,\ \gamma=-2.9^\circ,\ \phi=42^\circ,\ h=3\,\mathrm{km}\)): the exact projection matches the two code lines term by term — \(\dot\gamma\) is identical, and \(\dot\psi\) differs only by the dropped cross term \(\sim1.8\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{rad/s}\) (0.01% of the kept term).

项一：曲率俯仰，地面在脚下弯走Term 1: Curvature Pitch-up, the Ground Curves Away

飞机往前飞，当地“水平面”随地球弯下去。要保持相对地面同样的航迹角，\(\gamma\) 必须随之“抬头”——即使升力恰好等于重力。这一项总是正的（抬头），大小由前进速度和当地曲率决定。 As the aircraft moves forward, the local “horizon” bends downward with the Earth. To keep the same flight-path angle relative to the ground, \(\gamma\) must “pitch up” — even when lift exactly balances weight. This term is always positive (pitch-up); its size is set by forward speed and local curvature.

项二：子午线收敛，北向自己转Term 2: Meridian Convergence, North Rotates by Itself

不同经度上的“正北”在三维空间里并不平行（图 3 的灰虚线）。向东飞时，当地北向相对惯性空间缓慢转动，于是以北为基准的航向 \(\psi\) 也跟着变。这一项含 \(\tan\phi\)：越靠近极地，子午线越快收敛，效应越强；赤道上为零。 “True north” at different longitudes is not parallel in 3D space (the grey dashed line in Figure 3). Flying east, the local north slowly rotates relative to inertial space, so the heading \(\psi\) measured against north changes too. This term carries \(\tan\phi\): nearer the poles, meridians converge faster and the effect grows; it vanishes at the equator.

开关 include_transport 量化它有多重要。 脚本 geodetic_vs_reanchored_error.py 在 5 km 上比较：带输运项时，连续大地系统与逐步重锚 RK4 吻合到约 0.3 毫米；去掉输运项（include_transport=False）则漂移约 2.9 m 水平、1.3 m 高度、0.04° 航向——这正好等于输运率的累计。30 km 实验里，去输运（系统 D）约 145 m，带全项（系统 C）约 0.03 m。 The include_transport flag quantifies how much it matters. The script geodetic_vs_reanchored_error.py compares over 5 km: with the transport terms, the continuous geodetic system matches the per-step re-anchored RK4 to about 0.3 mm; dropping them (include_transport=False) drifts about 2.9 m horizontally, 1.3 m in altitude, and 0.04° in heading — exactly the accumulated transport rate. In the 30 km test, no-transport (system D) is ~145 m and the full RHS (system C) is ~0.03 m.

9. 终点：make_geodetic_dynamics_model 完整连续 RHSEnd: The Complete Continuous RHS of make_geodetic_dynamics_model

把变换 A（位置，第 6 节）和变换 B（输运，第 8 节）装进去，其余力学项原样保留，就得到直接写在大地坐标里的连续动力学。没有切平面，没有逐步重锚，只有一条光滑 \(f\)。现在两半（位置 + 输运）都齐了，先证明这条 RHS 正是第 7 节那个重锚步进器的连续极限（所以它既准又连续），再把它完整写出来。 Plug in Transformation A (position, Section 6) and Transformation B (transport, Section 8), keep the other force terms unchanged, and you get continuous dynamics written directly in geodetic coordinates. No tangent plane, no per-step re-anchoring — just one smooth \(f\). Now that both halves (position + transport) are in hand, we first prove this RHS is exactly the continuous limit of Section 7’s re-anchored stepper (so it is both accurate and continuous), then write it out in full.

数学证明：步进器的 \(\Delta t\to0\) 极限 = 连续大地 RHSProof: the \(\Delta t\to0\) Limit of the Stepper = the Continuous Geodetic RHS

先把步进器拆成三个映射的复合 \(\Phi_{\Delta t}=B\circ F\circ A\)，每个都看清楚——证明就水到渠成。 First write the stepper as a composition of three maps \(\Phi_{\Delta t}=B\circ F\circ A\); make each one explicit and the proof falls out.

\(A\)：大地 → ENU，锚定在当前点。因为锚点就是当前点，位置落在 ENU 原点，而且当前的速度方向角 \((\psi,\gamma)\) 在自己的当地框架里原封不动（这正是代码里的 x_enu0=(0,0,h,V,\psi,\gamma)）。 \(A\): geodetic → ENU, anchored at the current point. Because the anchor is the current point, the position lands at the ENU origin and the velocity angles \((\psi,\gamma)\) are unchanged in their own local frame (exactly the code’s x_enu0=(0,0,h,V,\psi,\gamma)).
\[ A(\mathbf x)=(\,\underbrace{0,0,h}_{\text{pos}},\;V,\psi,\gamma\,) \]
\(F\)：平直 RHS 走一个 RK4 步。平面里“走一米就是一米”，姿态只受气动项驱动（平直 RHS 没有输运项）。到一阶： \(F\): one RK4 step of the flat RHS. In the plane “one metre is one metre,” and the attitude is driven by the aerodynamic terms only (the flat RHS has no transport). To first order:
\[ (\delta E,\delta N,\delta U)=(V_E,V_N,V_U)\,\Delta t+O(\Delta t^2),\qquad \begin{aligned} \psi&\to\psi+\dot\psi_{\text{aero}}\,\Delta t\\ \gamma&\to\gamma+\dot\gamma_{\text{aero}}\,\Delta t \end{aligned} \]
\(B\)：ENU → 大地，但仍锚在老锚点。于是 \(B\) 要做两件事：(i) 把位移后的位置换回经纬度；(ii) 把速度重新投影到新点的当地框架（代码 enu_state_to_geodetic_expr 里的速度再投影）。下面证明 (i) 给出曲率半径项、(ii) 给出输运项。 \(B\): ENU → geodetic, still anchored at the old anchor. So \(B\) must do two things: (i) convert the displaced position back to lat/lon; (ii) reproject the velocity into the new point’s local frame (the velocity reprojection in enu_state_to_geodetic_expr). Below: (i) yields the curvature-radius terms, (ii) yields the transport terms.

位置（\(B\) 的第 i 件事）。把位移 \((\delta E,\delta N,\delta U)\) 经精确映射 \(G:\text{ENU}\to\text{geodetic}\)（ENU→大地）换回，在锚点（ENU 原点 \(\leftrightarrow\) 当前大地点）做一阶泰勒展开，其雅可比 \(J\) 就是第 7 节“绕一圈 vs 除以半径”那张卡片里的“除以半径”： Position (\(B\)’s task i). Map the displacement \((\delta E,\delta N,\delta U)\) back through the exact \(G=\text{ENU}\to\text{geodetic}\); Taylor-expand at the anchor (ENU origin \(\leftrightarrow\) current geodetic point), whose Jacobian \(J\) is the “divide by radius” from Section 7’s “round-trip vs divide-by-radius” card:

\[ \Phi_{\Delta t}(\mathbf x)\big|_{\text{pos}} =G(\delta E,\delta N,\delta U) =\underbrace{G(\mathbf 0)}_{=\,(\phi,\lambda,h)}+\,J\begin{pmatrix}\delta E\\\delta N\\\delta U\end{pmatrix}+O(\Delta t^2) \] \[ \Rightarrow\quad \lim_{\Delta t\to0}\frac{\Phi_{\Delta t}(\mathbf x)-\mathbf x}{\Delta t}\bigg|_{\text{pos}} =J\begin{pmatrix}V_E\\V_N\\V_U\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix}\dfrac{V_N}{R_M+h}\\[6pt]\dfrac{V_E}{(R_N+h)\cos\phi}\\[6pt]V_U\end{pmatrix} \]

这正是第 6 / 9 节的位置 RHS。This is exactly the position RHS of Sections 6 / 9.

姿态（\(B\) 的第 ii 件事）——输运项就在这里诞生。新点相对老锚点移动了一点，它的当地框架也随之转了一个小角度，正好是输运角速度乘步长 \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\Delta t+O(\Delta t^2)\)（\(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\) 见第 8 节）。\(F\) 只把姿态推进了气动率；\(B\) 把速度投影进这个转过 \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\Delta t\) 的新框架，读出的 \((\psi,\gamma)\) 就再加上第 8 节那两项输运增量： Attitude (\(B\)’s task ii) — this is where the transport terms are born. The new point has moved slightly, so its local frame has rotated by a small angle — exactly the transport angular velocity times the step, \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\Delta t+O(\Delta t^2)\) (\(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\) from Section 8). \(F\) advanced the attitude by the aero rates only; \(B\) projects the velocity into this frame rotated by \(\boldsymbol\omega_{en}\Delta t\), so the read-off \((\psi,\gamma)\) picks up exactly Section 8’s two transport increments:

\[ \psi_{\text{next}}=\psi+\bigl(\dot\psi_{\text{aero}}+\underbrace{\Delta\dot\psi}_{\text{transport}}\bigr)\Delta t+O(\Delta t^2), \qquad \gamma_{\text{next}}=\gamma+\bigl(\dot\gamma_{\text{aero}}+\underbrace{\Delta\dot\gamma}_{\text{transport}}\bigr)\Delta t+O(\Delta t^2) \]

除以 \(\Delta t\) 取极限，姿态率 = 气动 + 输运，正是本节下面完整 RHS 里的 \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\)。位置 + 姿态合起来：Divide by \(\Delta t\) and take the limit: the attitude rate = aero + transport — exactly the \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\) of this section’s full RHS below. Position and attitude together:

\[ \boxed{\;\lim_{\Delta t\to0}\frac{\Phi_{\Delta t}(\mathbf x,\mathbf u)-\mathbf x}{\Delta t}=f_{\text{geo}}(\mathbf x,\mathbf u)\;} \]
这一步把整页串起来了。 输运项不在 \(F\)（平直 RHS）里，它只从 \(B\) 的“投影进重锚后的新框架”冒出来。换句话说：连续大地 RHS 之所以必须显式加那两项输运，正是因为它要替代步进器每步偷偷做的这次重锚投影。曲率半径项同理——它替代 \(B\) 的位置换算。 This is what ties the whole page together. The transport terms are not in \(F\) (the flat RHS); they emerge only from \(B\)’s “projection into the re-anchored new frame.” In other words: the continuous geodetic RHS must add those two transport terms explicitly precisely because it replaces the re-anchoring projection the stepper does silently each step. Likewise the curvature-radius terms replace \(B\)’s position conversion.

所以步进器与连续大地 RHS 共享同一个连续向量场：步进器每步把坐标换算数值地算一遍，连续 RHS 把换算的导数（曲率半径 + 输运率）一次折进 \(f\)；有限 \(\Delta t\) 下两者只差 \(O(\Delta t^2)\) 的逐步截断。 So the stepper and the continuous geodetic RHS share one continuous vector field: the stepper computes the conversion numerically each step, the continuous RHS folds the conversion’s derivative (curvature radii + transport) into \(f\) once; at finite \(\Delta t\) they differ only by \(O(\Delta t^2)\) per-step truncation.

数值佐证。 \(J\) 与逐点数值换算吻合到约 10 位有效数字；脚本 geodetic_vs_reanchored_error.py 在 5 km 上测得：连续大地 RHS（带输运）与逐步重锚 RK4 仅差约 0.3 毫米——正是“同一连续向量场、不同离散算子”的实证。 Numerical evidence. \(J\) matches the pointwise numerical conversion to ~10 significant figures; the script geodetic_vs_reanchored_error.py measures, over 5 km, that the continuous geodetic RHS (with transport) and the per-step re-anchored RK4 differ by only about 0.3 mm — the same continuous vector field, realised by two different discrete operators.

七维状态 \((\phi,\lambda,h,V,\psi,\gamma,m)\) 的右端The Right-Hand Side of the 7-State \((\phi,\lambda,h,V,\psi,\gamma,m)\)

\[ \begin{aligned} \dot\phi &=\frac{V_N}{R_M+h} &\quad \dot\lambda &=\frac{V_E}{(R_N+h)\cos\phi} &\quad \dot h &=V_U \end{aligned} \] \[ \dot V=\frac{T-D}{m}-g\sin\gamma \] \[ \dot\psi=\underbrace{\frac{g\,n\sin\mu}{V\cos\gamma}}_{\text{aero (unchanged)}} \;\underbrace{-\;\frac{V\cos\gamma\cos\psi\tan\phi}{R_N+h}}_{\text{transport}} \] \[ \dot\gamma=\underbrace{\frac{g\,(n\cos\mu-\cos\gamma)}{V}}_{\text{aero (unchanged)}} \;\underbrace{+\;V\cos\gamma\!\left(\frac{\sin^2\psi}{R_M+h}+\frac{\cos^2\psi}{R_N+h}\right)}_{\text{transport}} \]

把它和第 3 节的平面 RHS 并排看：变的只有位置三行（米/秒 → 弧度/秒，引入 \(R_M,R_N,\cos\phi\)）和 \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\) 上各多出一项输运。\(\dot V\)、气动部分、阻力、密度全都没动。 Set this beside the flat RHS in Section 3: the only changes are the three position lines (metres/s → radians/s, introducing \(R_M,R_N,\cos\phi\)) and one extra transport term each on \(\dot\psi,\dot\gamma\). \(\dot V\), the aerodynamics, drag, and density are untouched. 

# aerodynamic_model/casadi_simulator.py — make_geodetic_dynamics_model(include_transport=True)

denom   = 1.0 - WGS84_E2 * sin_lat**2
R_N = WGS84_A / sqrt(denom)                  # prime-vertical radius
R_M = WGS84_A * (1.0 - WGS84_E2) / denom**1.5    # meridional radius

V_east  = V * cos_g * cos(psi)
V_north = V * cos_g * sin(psi)
V_up    = V * sin(gamma)

lat_dot = V_north / (R_M + h)                    # transformation A: position
lon_dot = V_east  / ((R_N + h) * cos(lat))
h_dot   = V_up

psi_dot   = g * n * sin(mu) / (V * cos_g)
gamma_dot = g * (n * cos(mu) - cos_g) / V

11. 为什么水平误差反而比高度误差大Why the Horizontal Error Is Larger Than the Altitude Error

直觉的陷阱：既然曲率表现为“切平面在竖直方向脱离椭球 \(\epsilon\approx L^2/2R\)”，那误差不该出现在高度上吗？但实测里平直 ENU（系统 A）水平差 ≈ 335 m、高度只差 ≈ 5.6 m。把这 335 m 拆开看更清楚：它几乎全是“沿航迹/里程”方向（系统 A 到得偏短约 335 m），横向只有 \(\sim\!0.3\) m。下面用真实代码把"为什么误差落在里程上、并且偏短"一步步讲清楚——结论是：不是重力变大，而是速度被算错了。 The intuitive trap: since curvature shows up as “the tangent plane departs vertically by \(\epsilon\approx L^2/2R\),” shouldn’t the error land in altitude? Yet flat ENU (system A) is off by ≈ 335 m horizontally and only ≈ 5.6 m in altitude. Decomposing the 335 m makes it clearer: it is almost entirely along-track (range) — system A arrives about 335 m short — with only \(\sim\!0.3\) m cross-track. Below we trace, with the actual code, why the error lands in range and why it is short. The conclusion: gravity does not grow; the speed is computed wrong.

实测分解（30 km，对照参考 B；脚本 dynamics_comparison_30km.py）：系统 A 的水平误差 = 沿航迹 −335 m、横向 \(\approx-0.3\) m；同时它一路比 B 慢，速度差累计到约 −2.6 m/s。高度差 −5.6 m、航向差 −0.04°。误差几乎是纯里程误差，且来自速度。 Measured decomposition (30 km, versus reference B; script dynamics_comparison_30km.py): system A’s horizontal error = −335 m along-track, \(\approx-0.3\) m cross-track; and it flies slower than B throughout, the speed gap reaching about −2.6 m/s. Altitude error −5.6 m, heading −0.04°. The error is almost pure range, and it comes from the speed.

第 1 步：误差不是重力变大，而是重力被参照到一组倾斜的坐标轴Step 1: The Error Is Not Gravity Growing — It Is Gravity Resolved Against Tilted Axes

重力没有变：始终大小 \(g\)、指向地心。问题在系统 A 把动力学积分在一块锚定在目标的切平面里——它的"水平/竖直"永远用目标处那一套轴。飞机飞出 \(L\) 之后，它真实的当地竖直已经和这块固定竖直差了一个倾角 \(\theta=L/R\)（30 km → \(\theta\approx0.27^\circ\)）。也就是说：同一个 \(g\)，被分解到了倾斜了 \(\theta\) 的坐标轴上。把这个"分解错位"投到真实的水平/竖直两个方向： Gravity does not change: always magnitude \(g\), pointing to Earth’s centre. The issue is that system A integrates the dynamics in a tangent plane anchored at the target — its "horizontal/vertical" always uses the axes at the target. After flying a distance \(L\), the aircraft’s true local vertical already differs from that fixed vertical by a tilt \(\theta=L/R\) (30 km → \(\theta\approx0.27^\circ\)). So the same \(g\) is being resolved against axes rotated by \(\theta\). Project that mis-resolution onto the true horizontal and vertical:

\[ \theta=\frac{L}{R} \] \[ a_{\text{horiz}}\approx g\sin\theta\approx g\,\theta \qquad(\textbf{一阶 / first order}) \] \[ a_{\text{vert}}\approx g\,(1-\cos\theta)\approx \tfrac12 g\,\theta^2 \qquad(\textbf{二阶 / second order}) \] \[ \frac{a_{\text{horiz}}}{a_{\text{vert}}}=\frac{\sin\theta}{1-\cos\theta}\approx\frac{2}{\theta} \;\approx\;425\;(\,L=30\,\text{km}\,) \]

那个著名的竖直缺口 \(\epsilon_U\approx L^2/2R=\tfrac12 R\theta^2\) 本身就是这个二阶余弦项。一阶的正弦项在沿速度方向留下 \(g\sin\theta\approx g\theta\approx 0.046\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\)（30 km）的分量，比竖直项大 \(\sim 2/\theta\approx 425\) 倍。但"差一个力"还不够——真正的关键是：这个力进了哪条方程、有没有东西能抵消它。第 2 步见分晓。 The famous vertical gap \(\epsilon_U\approx L^2/2R=\tfrac12 R\theta^2\) is this second-order cosine term. The first-order sine term leaves a component \(g\sin\theta\approx g\theta\approx 0.046\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\) (30 km) along the velocity, larger by \(\sim 2/\theta\approx 425\). But "a leftover force" is not the whole story — what really matters is which equation that force enters and whether anything cancels it. Step 2 settles that.

第 2 步：那个力进了"速度方程"，而升力不在这条方程里Step 2: That Force Enters the "Speed Equation," and Lift Is Not in It

速度大小 \(V\) 怎么变？看仿真器的速度方程（第 3 节）——它只受推力、阻力和重力沿速度的分量影响： How does the speed \(V\) change? Look at the simulator’s speed equation (Section 3) — it depends only on thrust, drag, and the component of gravity along the velocity:

\[ \dot V=\frac{T-D}{m}-\underbrace{g\sin\gamma}_{\text{gravity along }\hat{\mathbf v}} \qquad\bigl(\text{load factor }n\ \text{ is absent}\bigr) \]

航迹角 \(\gamma\) 在系统 A 里是相对那块固定（倾斜了 \(\theta\)）的水平面量的，于是 \(g\sin\gamma\) 被算错了约 \(g\theta\)。这正是第 1 步那个一阶分量——它就落在沿速度方向，直接进入 \(\dot V\)。致命之处：升力（载荷因子 \(n\)）垂直于速度，根本不出现在 \(\dot V\) 里，所以没有任何东西能抵消这个误差。 In system A the flight-path angle \(\gamma\) is measured against that fixed (tilted by \(\theta\)) horizontal, so \(g\sin\gamma\) is off by about \(g\theta\). This is exactly Step 1’s first-order component — it lies along the velocity and enters \(\dot V\) directly. The fatal part: lift (load factor \(n\)) is perpendicular to the velocity and never appears in \(\dot V\), so nothing cancels this error.

水平/沿航迹：速度被持续带偏 → 里程误差累积Horizontal / Along-Track: A Persistent Speed Bias → Accumulating Range Error

这点没人抵消的切向误差 \(\approx g\theta\) 一直在改 \(\dot V\)。实测系统 A 因此一路比 B 慢，速度差累计到约 \(-2.6\,\mathrm{m/s}\)。而沿航迹位置 \(=\int V\,dt\)：速度偏差被积分成不断增大的里程误差——30 km 处偏短约 335 m，横向几乎为 0。这正是惯性导航的经典链条：微小倾斜 → 切向比力算错 → 速度误差 → 位置误差。 This uncancelled tangential error \(\approx g\theta\) keeps biasing \(\dot V\). Measured, system A is slower than B throughout, the speed gap reaching about \(-2.6\,\mathrm{m/s}\). And along-track position \(=\int V\,dt\): the speed bias integrates into a growing range error — about 335 m short at 30 km, with ~0 cross-track. This is the classic inertial-navigation chain: a tiny tilt → wrong tangential specific force → velocity error → position error.

竖直/高度：有升力随时抵消 + 只到二阶 → 很小Vertical / Altitude: Lift Cancels It + Only Second Order → Small

竖直方向不一样：这里有一个随时再平衡的力——升力。它持续顶住当地重力的竖直分量，把倾斜误差大部分抵消；而倾斜泄漏进竖直本来就只有二阶的 \(\tfrac12 g\theta^2\)。再加上边界处 geodetic↔ENU 是精确旋转、高度又是直接携带的状态，于是高度误差只剩二阶残差（系统 A 仅 5.6 m）。一句话：竖直有升力回正，沿航迹的速度没有——所以倾斜永远先把误差倒进里程。 The vertical is different: here there is a continuously re-balancing force — lift. It keeps opposing the vertical component of local gravity, cancelling most of the tilt error; and the tilt leaks into the vertical only at second order \(\tfrac12 g\theta^2\). Add that the boundary geodetic↔ENU conversion is an exact rotation and altitude is a directly-carried state, and the altitude error is only a second-order residual (just 5.6 m for system A). In one line: the vertical has lift to restore it, the along-track speed does not — so a tilt always dumps the error into range first.

曲率误差落在哪个通道，取决于你做了哪种近似。 纯平直 ENU（系统 A）几乎把曲率误差全甩进水平（335 m 水平 / 5.6 m 高度）。而只缺输运项的大地模型（系统 D）位置运动学是对的，缺的恰好是竖直的“曲率俯仰”修正——于是那 \(L^2/2R\approx70\) m 反而显形在高度上（−69 m 高度 / 145 m 水平）。带全项的系统 C 两者都 ≈ 0。 Which channel the curvature error lands in depends on which approximation you make. Pure flat ENU (system A) dumps almost all of it into the horizontal (335 m horiz / 5.6 m alt). The geodetic model missing only the transport terms (system D) has correct position kinematics; what it lacks is exactly the vertical “curvature pitch-up,” so the \(L^2/2R\approx70\) m does surface in altitude (−69 m alt / 145 m horiz). The full system C is ≈ 0 in both.

  • 大地 RHS 内部用弧度；只有外层步进器对外用度。The geodetic RHS is radians inside; only the outer stepper exposes degrees.
  • 要复现重锚步进器，必须开 include_transport=True。To reproduce the re-anchored stepper, include_transport=True is required.
  • 输运项是几何效应，不是气动力矩；\(\tan\phi\) 在高纬变大、赤道为零。Transport terms are geometric, not aerodynamic; \(\tan\phi\) grows at high latitude and is zero at the equator.
