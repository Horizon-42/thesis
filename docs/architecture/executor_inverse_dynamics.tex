\paragraph{The point-mass equations.} The state is $(x, y, h, V, \psi, \gamma, m)$ and the controls are $(T, \varphi, n)$:
\begin{align}
\dot{x} &= V \cos\gamma \cos\psi, \\
\dot{y} &= V \cos\gamma \sin\psi, \\
\dot{h} &= V \sin\gamma, \\
\dot{V} &= \frac{T - D}{m} - g \sin\gamma, \\
\dot{\psi} &= \frac{g\, n \sin\varphi}{V \cos\gamma}, \\
\dot{\gamma} &= \frac{g\left(n \cos\varphi - \cos\gamma\right)}{V}, \\
\dot{m} &= 0.
\end{align}
The drag is $D = \tfrac{1}{2}\rho V^{2} C_D S$. The lift coefficient that the load factor needs is
$C_L = n\, m g / (\tfrac{1}{2}\rho V^{2} S)$, and $C_D = C_{D0} + k\, C_L^{2}$ plus a smooth stall term. Where $C_L$ would be
larger than $C_{L,\max}$, the wing stalls: $C_L = C_{L,\max}$, and the load factor that acts is
$n = \tfrac{1}{2}\rho V^{2} C_{L,\max} S / (m g)$. The air density is $\rho = \rho_0\,(1 - 0.0065\,h / 288.15)^{4.25588}$
with $\rho_0 = 1.225$\,kg/m$^3$ (ISA).

\paragraph{The inverse in one cycle.} Each cycle lasts 1\,s. The three laws of the executor give the wanted rates
$\dot{\chi}^{*}$, $\dot{\gamma}^{*}$ and $\dot{V}^{*}$. The compass track and the mathematical heading turn in opposite
directions, $\dot{\chi} = -\dot{\psi}$. Putting $\dot{\psi} = -\dot{\chi}^{*}$, $\dot{\gamma} = \dot{\gamma}^{*}$ and
$\dot{V} = \dot{V}^{*}$ into the equations above and solving for the controls gives the exact inverse:
\begin{align}
A &= n \sin\varphi = -\frac{\dot{\chi}^{*}\, V \cos\gamma}{g}, \\[4pt]
B &= n \cos\varphi = \frac{\dot{\gamma}^{*}\, V}{g} + \cos\gamma, \\[4pt]
\varphi &= \operatorname{atan2}(A,\, B), \qquad n = \frac{B}{\cos\varphi}, \\[4pt]
T &= m\left(\dot{V}^{*} + g \sin\gamma\right) + D(V,\, n,\, h).
\end{align}
$D(V, n, h)$ is the drag of the equations above at the load factor $n$ and the density at the height $h$.
