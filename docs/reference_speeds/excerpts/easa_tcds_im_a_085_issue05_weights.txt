Notes
  * No section prints a "minimum flight weight". Two minima are printed:
      - "Minimum zero fuel weight": 13,100 lb / 5,942 kg (HS.125-700A/B), 14,120 lb / 6,405 kg
        (800A/B, 800XP, 850XP, 900XP, 750), 16,550 lb (800 with Mod 25B047A);
      - "Minimum operating weight": 16,100 lb / 7,303 kg, printed for the 850XP and 900XP only.
  * Neither term is in the pack's min_mass_kind vocabulary (MFW/OEW/OWE/BOW/BEW/EW/SEW/BW). A minimum
    zero-fuel weight is a certified floor on the aircraft's mass without usable fuel, so (our reading, not
    printed) every in-flight mass of that model is at or above it; whether it may stand in for the row is
    the coordinator's call.
  * Lowest printed floor across the H25B models: 13,100 lb (5,942 kg), HS.125-700A/B, minimum zero fuel
    weight. Using the 850XP/900XP "Minimum operating weight" (16,100 lb) for the whole designator would
    put the floor above what the TCDS allows the 700/800/800XP/750 to weigh.
  * FAA TCDS A3EU Rev 44 prints the same numbers (excerpt faa_tcds_a3eu_rev44_weights.txt).
