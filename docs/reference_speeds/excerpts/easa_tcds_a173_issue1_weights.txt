2.3.10. Maximum Weights:
         From MSN 1 to 54
          Maximum ramp                           8 300 kg
          Maximum takeoff                        8 300 kg
          Maximum landing:                       7 800 kg
          Maximum zero fuel:                     5 650 kg
          Minimum flight                         4 500 kg

        From MSN 55, and MSN 1 to 54 fitted with AMD-BA service bulletin F10-0052, AFM
        DTM721 revision 12
         Maximum ramp                     8 500 kg
         Maximum takeoff                  8 500 kg
         Maximum landing:                 8 000 kg
         Maximum zero fuel:               6 150 kg
         Minimum flight                   4 500 kg

        From MSN 212 and MSN 1 to 211 fitted with AMD-BA service bulletin F10-0238, AFM
        DTM721 revision 28
         Maximum ramp                     8 800 kg
         Maximum takeoff                  8 755 kg
         Maximum landing:                 8 000 kg
         Maximum zero fuel:               6 540 kg
         Minimum flight                   4 500 kg

Notes
  * Minimum flight 4,500 kg in every column. The TCDS is issued "For model: Falcon 10"; ICAO FA10 also
    covers the Falcon 100 / Mystere 10 / 100 marketing names. Issue 1 dates from 2009 and was still the
    issue served by the EASA document library on 2026-09-23.
