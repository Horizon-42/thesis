[row 67, sheet note 2] 2- If there are dual values of AAC for a specific aircraft in the Flight Standardization Board (FSB), those values with their corresponding Approach Speed are reported in columns I, J, N, and O, respectively. There are airplanes in FSB for those there are dual values of AAC, while there is just one available Approach Speed corresponding to one of those two AACs. In these cases, the available Approach Speed is mentioned with its related AAC, then the missing Approach Speed is calculated based on its corresponding AAC such that the Approach Speed reflects its related AAC while it is the closest possible number to the available Approach Speed for the other AAC. In cases with dual AAC values, AAC in column H and its corresponding Approach Speed in column M depict the most critical (extreme) values of AAC and Approach Speed. For airplanes with a single value of AAC, AAC and its corresponding Approach Speed are reported in columns H and M, respectively.
