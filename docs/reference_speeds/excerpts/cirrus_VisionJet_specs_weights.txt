'Aircraft Specifications -> Weight' block. Retrieved 2026-09-07. Verbatim.

  Maximum Ramp Weight 6,040 lbs (2,740 kg)

  Basic Empty Weight 3,550 lbs (1,610 kg)
  Max Zero Fuel Weight 4,900 lbs (2,223 kg)
