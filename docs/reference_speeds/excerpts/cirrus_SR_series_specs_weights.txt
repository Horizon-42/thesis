Cirrus Aircraft, SR Series product page (https://cirrusaircraft.com/aircraft/sr22/),
'All Specs' block. Retrieved 2026-09-07. The one page carries the SR20, SR22 and SR22T
columns; the three are told apart by horsepower (215 / 310 / 315 hp). Verbatim.

  SR20   Horsepower 215 hp   Max Cruise Speed 155 ktas
         Useful Load 1,003 lbs   Max Gross Weight 3,150 lbs (1,429kg)   Base Weight 2,147 lbs (947 kg)

  SR22   Horsepower 310 hp   Max Cruise Speed 183 ktas
         Useful Load 1,328 lbs   Max Gross Weight 3,600 lbs (1,633kg)   Base Weight 2,272 lbs (1030 kg)

  SR22T  Horsepower 315 hp   Max Cruise Speed 213 ktas
         Useful Load 1,238 lbs   Max Gross Weight 3,600 lbs (1,633kg)   Base Weight 2,362 lbs (1071 kg)

  No landing weight and no approach speed are published on the page.
