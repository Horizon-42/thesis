Also read (PDF page 10, printed page 6), section 2.1 definitions:
  "Maximum Design Landing Weight (MLW). Maximum weight for landing as limited by aircraft
   strength and airworthiness requirements."
  "Operating Empty Weight (OEW). Weight of structure, powerplant, furnishing systems,
   unusable fuel and other unusable propulsion agents, and other items of equipment that
   are considered an integral part of a particular airplane configuration. Also included
   are certain standard items, personnel, equipment, and supplies necessary for full
   operations, excluding usable fuel and payload."
