Rules that protect the results.

  • The design documents set no test criteria. The user sets them after the design is settled (D7).
  • A readout that serves a choice of the user shows only the train and select days (D85).
  • A formal build or readout runs only where the user orders it.
  • No compatibility: a changed format gets a new name, and the code refuses an artefact of another name.

Limits that the design states.

  • Speed words are ground speeds. Wind can make a speed word at a turn that no controller said (open item O8).
  • The speed-word mask of the post-training acts in a narrow band (D101).
  • The recorded traffic of a scene lacks aircraft outside the 25 km slice and aircraft of other splits.
  • The observed slice ends before the threshold. The closed-loop reading gives no correction there (D44).
